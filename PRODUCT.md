# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Custom website hosted on Hostinger; not WordPress. Framework remains open, with Astro recommended for the eventual build.

## Users

Small-business owners worldwide, particularly plumbing businesses, recruiters, and outsourcing agencies. Visitors may hire Leo personally through the RankwithLeo agency.

## Product Purpose

RankwithLeo presents Leo's work and services, establishes trust through real client work, and converts qualified small-business visitors into strategy consultations or SEO audit requests.

## Positioning

Agentic SEO is the primary specialty. Supporting capabilities include website content creation, local SEO, custom website creation, business automation, basic app development, custom WordPress pages for client projects, and virtual assistance. Automation covers scoped workflows such as enquiry handoffs, reminders, data updates and reporting. Basic app development covers focused web tools and prototypes, not enterprise platforms or native mobile apps. Access, approvals, integrations, hosting and maintenance are agreed per project. The business is founder-led: clients work directly with Leo while benefiting from an agent-assisted delivery model.

## Operating Context

The portfolio must explain services in practical business terms, show completed work through case studies, and give visitors a clear path to discuss an engagement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site needs a landing page, work/case studies, and the supporting pages required for credibility and conversion.
- The primary conversion is a strategy consultation; an SEO opportunity audit is the lower-commitment alternative.
- The website will target a worldwide audience.
- The final website must be custom-built and hosted on Hostinger, not built with WordPress.

## Brand Commitments

- Agency name: RankwithLeo.
- Founder-led presentation: visitors can hire Leo personally through the agency.
- Leo remains responsible for strategy and judgment; Codex is the primary agentic tool used to assist delivery. Do not position the work as Claude-led.
- Agentic SEO must remain the primary capability rather than appearing as one undifferentiated service among many.
- Jordan James Media was supplied as a structural and positioning reference, not as copy or a visual identity to reproduce.

## Evidence on Hand

The selected-work section is limited to these two client companies:

- AntonsPlumbingAndGas.com.au
- AntonsRenovations.com.au

Public-use permission, project screenshots, project details, testimonials, logos, and measurable outcomes still need confirmation. Future work must not invent performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Lead with Agentic SEO and business outcomes, then reveal the supporting services.
- Make Leo visibly accountable for the strategy, judgment, and delivered work.
- Let real projects and verifiable evidence carry the credibility.
- Give small-business owners a simple path from understanding the offer to starting a conversation.
- Keep claims specific and honest; label unavailable metrics rather than replacing them with vanity language.
